Authorized use only

You may only scan websites that you own or are explicitly authorized to assess. By submitting a scan you confirm you have that authorization.

What the scanner does

The free scanner performs passive checks: it requests your home page over HTTP and HTTPS and inspects the TLS certificate and response headers. It does not attempt to log in, submit forms or exploit vulnerabilities.

No warranty

Results are provided as-is for informational purposes. A clean result does not guarantee that a website is secure, and the scan is not a substitute for a professional penetration test.

Fair use

Scans are rate-limited. Automated or abusive use of the scanner may be blocked.
