    After a breach

    The four things that matter most

    Unique passwords

    Use a password manager so one breach can't unlock your other accounts.

    Two-factor login

    Turn on 2FA for email, banking and social accounts. An authenticator app beats SMS.

    Watch for phishing

    Leaked data makes scam emails convincing. Never click links in unexpected "security" messages.

    Credit freeze

    If government ID or financial data leaked, freezing your credit stops new accounts in your name.
